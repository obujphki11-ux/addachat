AddaChat — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

AddaChat ("the app") is a messaging app developed by Nayem Islam. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using AddaChat you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use these to create your AddaChat account and profile.
  • Profile information. Anything you add to your profile, such as an About text or a profile photo.
  • Messages and content you send. Text messages, photos, voice messages, videos and files you send through the app are stored on our servers (Google Firebase) so they can be delivered to the people you chat with and synced when you sign in again.
  • Calls. Voice and video calls are connected directly between devices (peer-to-peer using WebRTC). Call audio and video are not recorded and are not stored by us.

How we use information

  • To provide messaging, voice and video calling features.
  • To show your profile and online/typing status to the people you chat with.
  • To keep the service safe — for example, an administrator may remove accounts that abuse the service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is processed by Google Firebase (database, storage and authentication) only as needed to run the app, under Google's own privacy and security terms. The people you message can see the messages and media you send them.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app's Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile and account data from our servers. Content you already sent to other people may remain visible in their chats.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. We work to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

AddaChat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with its effective date shown at the top.
