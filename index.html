How to install — step by step

1.  Tap the green Download button — the APK file will save

2.  Open the downloaded file (notification or Downloads folder)

3.  If asked, Allow installs from this source

4.  If Play Protect appears, follow the box below

5.  Tap Install, then Open and sign in with Google

If Play Protect stops the install

1.  Open the Play Store and tap your profile photo (top right)

2.  Tap Play Protect, then the gear icon (top right)

3.  Turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect" and tap Turn off

4.  Come back and install AddaChat again

You can turn the scan back on after installing.
