Privacy Policy — YT Live Streamer

Effective date: 11 November 2026  •  Developer: Nayem Islam

YT Live Streamer lets you broadcast a video file from your phone to your own YouTube or Facebook account through our streaming server. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

  • Google account basics — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. They identify your account inside the app and are shown to the app administrator.
  • Device identifier — a device ID is stored with your account so that one account works on one phone, as the service rules require.
  • Videos you upload — a video you choose is uploaded to our streaming server only so it can be broadcast to the platform and stream key you provide. Uploaded files are used for your live session and are not sold or shared.
  • Stream keys — the stream key you type is sent to the streaming server to start your broadcast. Do not share your stream key with anyone.
  • Usage counters — we record how many rewarded ads you watched and how much live time you used, so your live credit balance can be calculated.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and account data storage.
  • Google AdMob — the app shows rewarded ads. Google may collect an advertising ID and ad-interaction data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube / Facebook — your broadcast is delivered to the platform account whose stream key you provide; that platform's own privacy policy applies to the broadcast itself.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, messages, or files other than the single video you choose to upload.
  • We do not keep your Google password — sign-in happens entirely through Google.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted at any time by emailing us; the administrator can also delete an account from the admin panel.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
